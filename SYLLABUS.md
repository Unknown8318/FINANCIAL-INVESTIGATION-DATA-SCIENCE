# Syllabus

13 weeks, approximately 10 hours per week; advancement is competency-based. Each module combines theory, guided coding, independent work and assessment.

## Week 1: Orientation and financial investigation
Deliverable: Initial transaction review.

## Week 2: Python fundamentals
Deliverable: Transaction summaries.

## Week 3: Advanced Python and pandas
Deliverable: Data cleaning pipeline.

## Week 4: Exploratory data analysis
Deliverable: Transaction dashboard.

## Week 5: Fraud classification
Deliverable: Validated fraud classifier.

## Week 6: Statistics and regression
Deliverable: Financial behavior analysis.

## Week 7: Feature engineering and ensembles
Deliverable: Risk scoring.

## Week 8: Anomaly detection
Deliverable: Unusual activity detection.

## Week 9: Network and graph analytics
Deliverable: Financial relationship mapping.

## Week 10: Cryptocurrency investigation
Deliverable: Public ledger analysis.

## Week 11: APIs and automation
Deliverable: Data collection pipeline.

## Week 12: SQL and deployment
Deliverable: Financial intelligence database.

## Week 13: Capstone investigation
Deliverable: Operation Paper Trail.

Week 01 grading: setup 10, exercises 15, case 55, quiz 20. Target 80/100 and correct material errors before advancement. Later rubrics will accompany their modules.
