# Operation Paper Trail — Intake

A fictional financial intelligence unit needs a reproducible review of the supplied sample, not a conclusion of wrongdoing. Create submissions/case_review.py using the standard library.

1. Load case_transactions.csv without changing it. Print row count; check unique IDs and positive amounts.
2. Print total volume and largest transfer (ID and amount).
3. List IDs with amounts at least $5,000, an arbitrary classroom rule.
4. Calculate incoming and outgoing totals for two accounts and explain why you chose them.
5. Compare activity to account profiles: identify one plausible ordinary business explanation and one lead that warrants more context. Manual comparison is acceptable.
6. Record run command, Python version and output in the report.

Write 200–400 words with at least three transaction IDs, observations vs hypotheses, alternative explanations, missing information, limitations and confidence. No unsupported allegations. Optional: compare workload under thresholds of $3,000 and $7,000.

Rubric (55): integrity 10, totals and maximum 10, screening 10, account flows 10, qualified assessment 15.
